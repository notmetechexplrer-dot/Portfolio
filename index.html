About Me

I am a CSE (AI & Data Science) student at MIT Academy of Engineering, dedicated to preserving human problem-solving capabilities in an automated era. Rather than treating frameworks and AI models as black boxes, I trace technology back to its underlying hardware, computational theory, and mathematical principles.
